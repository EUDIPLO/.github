# Governance

EUDIPLO is governed by its
[technical charter](CHARTER.pdf).
This page summarizes it; if the two differ, the charter applies.

## Roles

- **Contributors** are anyone who contributes code, documentation or other
  technical artifacts.
- **Maintainers** are contributors who can merge changes into a repository.
  Each repository lists its maintainers in `MAINTAINERS.md`.

## Technical Steering Committee

The Technical Steering Committee (TSC) is responsible for the technical
oversight of the project: its technical direction, releases, security and
contribution policies, and creating or archiving repositories. The voting
members of the TSC are the project's maintainers. TSC meetings are open to the
public; the community call is the usual place for them.

## Decisions

The project aims for consensus. Day-to-day decisions are made in issues and pull
requests. When a vote is needed, each voting member has one vote:

- At a meeting, quorum is at least half of the voting members, and a decision
  needs a majority of those present.
- An electronic vote without a meeting needs a majority of all voting members.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor becomes a maintainer by majority approval of the existing
maintainers. A maintainer can be removed by majority approval of the other
maintainers.

## Organization settings

GitHub teams and repository permissions are managed as code in the
[governance repository](https://github.com/EUDIPLO/governance). To add a
repository or change a team, open a pull request there.
